Romaji → Kana

Type Japanese in romaji, get hiragana or katakana back — sokuon, ん, and long vowels handled automatically.

Handles doubled consonants (kitte → きって), ん before consonants or word-end, "nn"/"n'" for explicit ん before a vowel, and — in katakana — contracts repeated vowels into the long-vowel mark (kyuuto → キュート). A couple of very common words (konnichiwa, konbanwa) are spelled with は rather than わ, matching real usage.

Mandarin pinyin → hanzi isn't included: unlike kana, pinyin syllables map to many unrelated characters (ma → 妈/麻/马/骂), so it needs a dictionary and a candidate picker rather than a direct conversion.
